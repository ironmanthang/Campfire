### A private journal app, with local AI intergrated in it, so none of your journals can get to third party provider.

whatsup people! I wanted to share a side project I've been building called Campfire. Oh yeah btw it's completely free on the Microsoft Store, no paywalls or subscription fees.
It's a cozy digital journal that covers all the core essentials - Markdown preview, timeline browsing, tagging, and fast search. But I wanted to take it a step further by sprinkling in a little local AI for private self-reflection : >

Key Highlights:
- **Semantic Search**: Search your journal entries by meaning rather than just exact keywords using local embedding models.
- **Campfire Chat**: Chat directly with a local LLM that has context over your past entries. You can ask it to summarize recent logs, spot patterns in your mood, help you brainstorm when facing writer's block, or just casually chat privately with it since it's a local model :)
- **100% Private & Flexible**: Runs fully local via Ollama so your intimate diary entries never leave your device. (And if your hardware is low-spec, you can connect to Ollama cloud models).

I built this with Tauri + React. You can install the native desktop app or try the web PWA instantly.

Here are the links, I'd really appreciate it if you could spend a little time to test it out and review my app:
- **Microsoft Store (Windows Native App)**: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p40g2gtc6gg
- **Web PWA (Add to phone home screen)**: https://app-campfire.pages.dev/

P.S. I vibe-coded this with the goofy Gemini 3.6 Flash, so if you spot any UI/UX bugs, it'd be nice if you give feedback to me directly in the app!
